Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 25, 2026

Milk Mondays (“we”, “us”, or “our”) operates the Milk Mondays website. This policy explains how we handle information when you visit the site, read the archive, use the email access gate, leave comments, or use linked services.

Information you provide

We may receive information you choose to provide, such as your name and email address when you use the email access gate, submit a story, contact us, or use a linked form. If you post a comment, we process the email address used for access and the public display name and comment text you submit.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting and security providers may process technical information such as IP address, browser and device information, pages requested, and security or performance logs. The site also uses browser storage to remember access-gate status, saved articles, and likes on your device.

How we use information

We use information to provide access to the archive, send requested one-time verification codes, operate and moderate comments, respond to messages, maintain site security, improve reliability, and meet legal obligations.

Service providers and linked services

We use Cloudflare to host and secure the site and to support site features. We use Brevo to send one-time access-code emails. Linked forms, Patreon, Instagram, and other third-party destinations are operated under their own privacy policies. Please review those policies before providing information through those services.

Advertising and cookies

When Google AdSense ads are enabled, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, or similar technologies to serve and measure ads, prevent fraud, and, where permitted, personalise advertising. We will provide any required consent choices through an appropriate consent message. You can also manage cookies through your browser settings; some site features may not work as intended if browser storage is disabled.

For more information about how Google uses data when you use sites or apps that use Google services, visit Google’s partner-sites policy.

How long we keep information

We keep information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described here, including access, moderation, security, recordkeeping, and legal obligations. You can clear information stored in your browser through your browser settings.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, or to object to certain processing. To make a privacy request, contact us using the email below. We may need to verify your request before responding.

Children’s privacy

Milk Mondays is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can review and address the request.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our practices or services change. We will post the updated version on this page and revise the effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email milkmondaysbiz@gmail.com.
